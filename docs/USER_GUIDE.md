# Using Crossfade

Crossfade puts Spotify and YouTube behind one search bar and one player. This guide walks through everything you
can do with it, from a first search to a playlist you share with friends.

## Without an account

You can use most of Crossfade without signing in: search both platforms, play anything you find, paste links, and
open public playlists people share with you. Signing in adds playlists, Liked Songs, history and imports.

## Signing in

Choose **Sign in** and pick Spotify or Google. Either one gives you a Crossfade account. You can link the other
later under **Settings → Connected accounts**, and from then on either one signs you in.

- **Spotify** is what lets you import your Spotify playlists and, with Premium, play full Spotify tracks right in
  the browser.
- **Google** is what lets you import your YouTube playlists. Crossfade only asks to see your YouTube library when
  you go to import, not when you sign in.

## Searching

Type in the search bar at the top (press <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> to jump there). Results from
both platforms come back together, each marked with a Spotify or YouTube badge. Use the **Spotify** and
**YouTube** chips to see one platform at a time.

You can also paste a link instead of searching:

- a Spotify track, album or playlist (`open.spotify.com/...` or `spotify:track:...`)
- a YouTube video or playlist, including youtu.be, Shorts and music.youtube.com links

Albums and playlists open as a list you can play, shuffle, or save as a Crossfade playlist of your own.

Not sure what to look for? The chips under **Start with a vibe** run a search for you.

## Playing music

Double-click a track to play it, or press the play button that appears on it. On a phone, tap anywhere on the
row. Whatever list you started
from (search results, a playlist, an album, Liked Songs) keeps playing in order, and the player switches between
Spotify and YouTube on its own.

The player bar along the bottom has the usual controls: shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat (off, all, one),
the progress bar and volume. On the right are:

- **Now playing view**: the artwork, or the video for YouTube tracks, plus details about the track
- **Queue**: what's playing and what's next
- **Crossfade** (the amber icon): how long tracks blend into each other, from 0 (no blend) to 12 seconds. The
  picture shows the two volume curves for the length you pick. Tracks that run straight on from each other on the
  same album aren't blended, so live albums and mixes play the way they were made.

### What you'll hear from Spotify

How a Spotify track plays depends on your account:

- **Signed in with Spotify Premium:** full tracks, through Spotify's player in the browser.
- **Anyone else:** the Spotify embed, which plays full tracks if you're signed in at spotify.com in the same
  browser, and 30-second previews if you aren't.

YouTube videos always play in full. The video needs to stay visible while it plays (YouTube's rule), so it shows in
the now playing view or a small dock in the corner.

### The queue

Open any track's **⋯** menu (or right-click the row) and choose **Add to queue** or **Play next**. Queued tracks
play before the rest of the list, and then the list carries on where it left off. Remove a queued track from the
queue panel, or click any upcoming track there to jump to it.

## Playlists

Make one with the **+** next to **Your library**, then **New playlist**. To add a track, open its **⋯** menu and
choose **Add to playlist**, which also offers a new playlist right there. A playlist can hold Spotify tracks and
YouTube videos side by side, up to 2,000 of them.

On a playlist you own:

- **Reorder** by dragging the handle that appears to the left of a track when you point at it (on a computer or
  tablet).
- **Remove** a track from its **⋯** menu.
- **Rename it, add a description or change the cover** by clicking the title or the cover. Covers can be JPEG, PNG
  or WebP photos; Crossfade crops them to a square. Without one, the cover is made from the tracks' artwork.
- **Delete** it from the **⋯** menu next to the play button.

## Sharing

On a playlist you own, choose the **Share** button next to shuffle (it's also in the **⋯** menu).

- **Who can listen:** _Private_ playlists are only for you and the people you invite. _Public_ ones open for anyone
  with the link, even without an account, and appear on your profile.
- **People:** type someone's username to invite them. Collaborators can add, remove and reorder tracks. Renaming,
  deleting and changing who it's shared with stay with you. Remove someone from the same list.
- **Copy link** to send it around.

If someone shares a playlist with you, it shows up in your library. You can leave it from its **⋯** menu.

Your username is in **Settings**, and your profile at `sleeep.dev/crossfade/user/<username>` lists your public
playlists.

## Importing from Spotify and YouTube

Choose **Import from Spotify or YouTube** from the **+** menu in your library.

- **Spotify:** pick any playlist you created or collaborate on. Spotify doesn't let apps read playlists you only
  follow, so those are listed separately and can't be imported.
- **YouTube:** allow access to your YouTube library when asked, then pick a playlist, or your Liked videos.

An import is a copy. It becomes an ordinary Crossfade playlist you can edit and share, and later changes on Spotify
or YouTube don't carry over.

## Liked Songs and history

Press the heart on any track to save it to **Liked Songs**, at the top of your library. Anything you play for more
than 30 seconds (or half of a short track) is remembered, and the home page uses that for **Jump back in** and
**Recently played**.

## On your phone

Crossfade works in a phone browser too. The tabs along the bottom are **Home**, **Search** and **Your library**.
The current track sits just above them; tap it to open the full-screen player, and swipe it down (or tap the arrow)
to close it. The **Queue** button is at the bottom of the full-screen player.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere to see this list. Shortcuts work everywhere except while you're typing.

| Keys                                         | Action                     |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd>             | Play or pause              |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>                | Next track                 |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>P</kbd>                | Previous track             |
| <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>L</kbd>                  | Back or forward 10 seconds |
| <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd>                  | Back or forward 5 seconds  |
| <kbd>M</kbd>                                 | Mute or unmute             |
| <kbd>S</kbd>                                 | Shuffle                    |
| <kbd>R</kbd>                                 | Repeat                     |
| <kbd>Q</kbd>                                 | Show or hide the queue     |
| <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> | Search                     |

Media keys on your keyboard and the controls on your lock screen work too.

## When something doesn't play

- **A Spotify track only plays 30 seconds.** You're hearing the preview. Sign in at spotify.com in this browser, or
  sign in to Crossfade with a Spotify Premium account.
- **"Connect Spotify" or "Reconnect".** Your Spotify link expired or was revoked. Choose it to sign in to Spotify
  again; your playlists are unaffected.
- **A video is unavailable.** Its owner has turned off playback on other sites, or it's private, deleted or
  blocked in your country. Crossfade skips to the next track.
- **YouTube results are missing.** YouTube limits how many searches Crossfade can make each day. Spotify results
  still work, and YouTube comes back after midnight Pacific time. Pasting a YouTube link still works in the
  meantime.
